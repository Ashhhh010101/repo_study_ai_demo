# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private data.
- Help make technical discussions accessible to new contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberate security abuse, credential exposure, or attempts to bypass coordinated disclosure.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this policy. Reports should be made privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile or, for security-related incidents, through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and fairly as practical.
